A friendly guide to your Cabo property search

Do I need to sign up to search?

No. Browse listings, view photos, save favorites and compare properties without registering. Contact us whenever you are ready for help.

Can I search directly by subdivision?

Yes. Choose a subdivision if you already know where you want to look. Or begin with a zone, then narrow your search by area, community and subdivision. Changing a location clears incompatible choices below it.

Where are my saved properties kept?

Click Save property on a listing to keep up to 50 favorites in this browser. Open Saved & Compare to return to them later. These browser-only favorites are not shared across devices, and clearing browser data removes them. You can also sign in to a free buyer account to save favorites and named searches across devices. Private browsing may remove them when you close the session.

How do I compare properties?

Open Saved & Compare, select two or three saved properties, then choose Compare selected to see them side by side.

Which listings and locations can I search?

Search the complete active MLS inventory across Baja California Sur through our authorized MLS BCS feed, including listings from participating brokerages throughout the state. Use the property type and location filters to explore houses, condos, land and other available property types. Private and off-market properties are not included.

Listings displayed courtesy of the IDX program of MLS BCS. Listings may be represented by other offices. All information is deemed reliable but not guaranteed.
