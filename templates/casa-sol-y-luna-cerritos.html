The freedom of separate spaces

A home that can change
with your plans.

Some properties give you one way to live. Casa Sol y Luna gives you several. A main home and two separate casitas let you keep a private place of your own while making room for family, friends or a rental arrangement.

Built and developed by a craftsman over the years, the property has a character that is unmistakably Baja: palapa roofs, custom wood details, native gardens and terraces that draw your attention back to the Pacific.

Its El Gavilán setting lies between the Cerritos and San Pedrito surf areas. The listing describes 180-degree ocean views and a sandy beach about a four-minute walk away.

More than a bedroom count

Three ways to make it your own.

Keep your own private retreat.

Use the main home for yourself. When friends or family visit, the separate casitas give them their own space and let everyone enjoy time together without sharing every room.

Buy now. Move here later.

If Baja is part of your retirement plans, the three independent living spaces offer the possibility of rentals while you prepare for the move. Ask us about the practical arrangements and current local rental demand.

Live here with options.

Once you make the main home your own, keep the casitas for guests, explore renting one or both, or adapt how you use them over time. Separate kitchens and living spaces are what make that flexibility possible.

Rental use, expenses and any permissions should be reviewed for your plans. Rental income and future property values are not guaranteed.

Open living, custom wood details and views toward the ocean.

The Baja character is already here

Sea views, open air
and room to create.

The main home combines open living areas, ocean-facing terraces and an upstairs studio space. Palapa roofs and individual finishes give the buildings a warm, personal feel.

  • Independent casitas with their own kitchens and living spaces
  • Adjustable-current swimming and solar pool heating, as described in MLS
  • Terraces, outdoor gathering areas and native gardens
  • Garages, workshop and enclosed storage
  • CFE electricity, a hybrid solar system and water filtration

There is also land to discuss for future expansion. Walk the property with us to understand the boundaries, existing buildings and what might be possible.
