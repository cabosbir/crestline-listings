Why are we showing only completed properties?

We start with completed properties so you can inspect the actual residence, compare its condition and surroundings, and see which services and amenities are operating. This reduces exposure to construction delays, unfinished amenities and a developer’s need for future sales or progress payments to finish the project.

A completed label is not a guarantee: confirm the property’s condition, documents, utilities and any HOA obligations. We consider preconstruction selectively, with particular attention to the developer’s record of completing projects and financial capacity to deliver through slower sales or rising costs.

Buying in a Los Cabos golf community: membership questions

Does buying a property include golf?

Confirm the specific residence’s eligibility and the club’s approval process. An MLS golf-community label, a course view or an owner discount does not establish that membership is included or transfers on resale.

Private club or pay as you play?

Consider how many months you will be in Cabo, the rounds you expect to play and whether your family will use other club facilities. Compare member booking priority and guest privileges with available public or homeowner round rates.

What belongs in the budget?

Separate the initiation payment or deposit, annual dues, transfer costs, per-round charges and HOA expenses. Ask which costs are required, which are optional, what currency applies and whether taxes are included.

Which fees are published?

Cabo San Lucas Country Club displays membership prices. Diamante publishes a dated seasonal tee-time policy. Most private-club guides need a current individual quote; advertised round rates are not membership dues.

Tell Don how you want to use the property

Share your preferred area, budget, months in Cabo, expected rounds and family priorities. Don can help narrow the properties and identify the club terms to confirm before you buy.
