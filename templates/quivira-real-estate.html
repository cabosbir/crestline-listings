Quivira Los Cabos: Golf, Ocean Views & Luxury Living on Cabo’s Pacific Coast

By Hector Mendoza, BIR real estate advisor · Hector’s community guide · October 14, 2026

There’s another side of Cabo San Lucas that many visitors never get to experience. A place where the desert meets the Pacific Ocean, championship golf overlooks dramatic coastal cliffs, and luxury homes offer a peaceful escape just minutes from downtown Cabo.

Welcome to Quivira Los Cabos, one of the most distinctive residential communities on the Pacific side of Baja California Sur.

Why Live on the Pacific Side of Cabo?

While Cabo San Lucas is famous for its marina, nightlife, and Médano Beach, the Pacific side offers something different: open landscapes, incredible sunsets, beautiful ocean views, and a more relaxed residential atmosphere.

Quivira combines this peaceful setting with convenient access to downtown Cabo, where residents can enjoy restaurants, shopping, entertainment, and everyday services.

It’s an ideal combination for anyone looking for a vacation home, retirement residence, or a place to spend more time enjoying the Baja lifestyle.

World-Class Golf at Your Doorstep

One of Quivira’s biggest attractions is its Jack Nicklaus Signature Golf Course, an 18-hole championship course carved into the dramatic cliffs overlooking the Pacific Ocean.

Recognized by Golf Digest among the world’s greatest golf courses, Quivira offers spectacular views and a unique golfing experience. See our golf guide for course details and a dated ranking.

Quivira has also announced plans for a second Jack Nicklaus-designed championship golf course, which would further expand the community’s golfing experience. We have not confirmed its current construction timeline or opening date.

For eligible homeowners, one of the advantages is access to preferred golf rates, making it easier to enjoy one of Cabo’s most remarkable courses.

Resort Amenities and Exclusive Owner Benefits

Quivira is more than a residential development. It’s a resort-connected community associated with the renowned Pueblo Bonito family of resorts.

A major advantage of Quivira residency is access arrangements with participating Pueblo Bonito resorts within Quivira, as well as Pueblo Bonito Rosé and Pueblo Bonito Los Cabos (Blanco) on downtown Cabo’s Médano Beach. These resort privileges are associated with Quivira residency rather than a particular residential neighborhood, with eligibility and use of individual facilities subject to current owner-program terms and applicable resort policies.

Residents can enjoy resort experiences that may include swimming pools, fitness facilities, restaurants, and spa services, along with special owner discounts and access arrangements. The Quivira lifestyle brings together the privacy of owning a home with the convenience of resort-style amenities.

Find the Community That Fits Your Lifestyle

Quivira features several residential neighborhoods, each offering something unique.

  • Copala: Hacienda-inspired condominiums and single-family houses with a private clubhouse, pools, fitness center, and recreational amenities.
  • Mavila: A village-style neighborhood featuring condominiums and single-family residences.
  • Coronado: Exclusive luxury estates with impressive golf course and Pacific Ocean views.
  • Alvar and Old Lighthouse Club: Upscale residential options offering distinctive architecture and spectacular coastal settings.

From low-maintenance condominiums to spacious luxury homes, Quivira offers opportunities for a variety of lifestyles and preferences.

Discover Your Home on Cabo’s Pacific Coast

Imagine morning coffee overlooking the ocean, afternoons enjoying championship golf or resort amenities, and evenings watching the Pacific sunset from your own terrace.

What should you compare in Quivira?

Compare the actual drive into Cabo and the entrance for the property you are considering. Do not treat all Pacific-side addresses as the same location.

Check the services and amenities included with the property. Golf facilities, resort services and beach access may have separate conditions.

What does the condo really cost to own?

Ask for current HOA dues, the approved budget, reserves and any announced assessments. Confirm parking, storage, pets and rental rules for the actual regime and phase. Compare the building and shared systems as carefully as the unit.

Which unit is right for your use?

Check the floor, stairs or elevator access, terrace orientation and noise at the time of day you expect to be home. Verify which listing photographs show the unit being sold and which show common areas. Tell Don whether this is for full-time living, seasonal use or another purpose.

What should you inspect beyond the living area?

Compare roof and exterior condition, cooling, water storage, electrical systems and drainage. Consider who will maintain the property when you are away. A private pool and garden can add enjoyment as well as ongoing costs.

How does the house fit daily life?

Walk the stairs, check parking and storage, and test the route to everyday shopping. If a house is in a managed community, ask about its rules, dues and any separate club arrangements. Compare the finished property with your actual needs, not simply its bedroom count.

What can you actually build?

Start with a survey, the parcel’s legal status, permitted use and any community design requirements. Ask qualified professionals to assess setbacks, terrain, access and restrictions for the project you intend. An attractive asking price does not answer these questions.

What belongs in the full project budget?

Account for professional work, approvals, utility connections, access improvements and terrain-related construction. Compare the total estimated project and timetable with completed alternatives. Keep the land purchase decision separate from assumptions about future resale value.

Prepare for the next step

Compare suitable properties with a clear budget and intended use, then request the documents and inspections relevant to the purchase.
