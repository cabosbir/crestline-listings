What should you verify in every condo community?

  • Current dues, reserves, planned assessments and what the HOA maintains.
  • Parking and storage rights, stairs or elevator access and usable terrace space.
  • Water supply, shared storage, maintenance and utility expenses.
  • Rental and pet rules, management arrangements and actual rental records where available.
  • Facilities already operating, separate memberships and any ongoing construction.
  • The residence’s documents and completed condition, rather than relying on a development name or rendering.

For a finished home, begin with completed units and verify readiness. Consider preconstruction only after reviewing delivery capability, funding and a demonstrated completion record with appropriate professional advice.
