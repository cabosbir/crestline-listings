Local guidance from Don Weis, BIR broker

Compare the building as carefully as the condo

El Tezal offers a wide choice of condos near Cabo San Lucas, but water arrangements, monthly costs, access and future views can differ substantially between buildings. Use the listings to narrow your choices, then investigate the specific unit and development.

Why focus on completed condos?

You can inspect the residence, its surroundings and the services and amenities actually operating. This avoids relying solely on a promised delivery date or future facilities.

A completed unit can still sit within an unfinished phase. Confirm access, utilities, occupancy readiness, elevators, parking and shared facilities; the MLS Completed label alone does not establish these.

Bring the evidence into the comparison

Ask for recent HOA statements, the approved operating budget, water expenses, reserves and any additional assessments. Visit the actual unit and look at neighboring sites.

Our agents can help you identify questions and obtain information for your shortlist. A good-looking listing is the beginning of that review.

Who pays for trucked water in an El Tezal condo?

Water supply and delivery costs deserve a place in your buying budget. Ask whether the development depends partly or entirely on trucked water, how much it uses and what owners actually pay.

Shared water costs become owners’ monthly costs

When a condominium development buys trucked water, the HOA pays the cost and shares it among owners through monthly charges. Check whether your quoted HOA dues already cover those deliveries or whether water is billed as an additional monthly charge.

For a freestanding house with its own water arrangements, the owner pays directly. If the house is in a managed community, confirm which water costs are shared and which are the owner’s responsibility.

Ask for the actual bills

  • Review the condo’s HOA statements and the development’s water expenses for the last 6–12 months.
  • Ask how the water charge is allocated to your unit and whether it changes month to month.
  • Check whether extra deliveries have led to additional collections or assessments.

Check supply and storage

  • Confirm the current water source, delivery arrangements and available storage.
  • Ask how interruptions are handled and who maintains pumps, tanks and distribution systems.
  • Understand whether pools, landscaping and other shared facilities affect the water budget.

There is no single water-cost figure that fits every El Tezal condo. Compare documented charges for the specific building and unit, rather than assuming the advertised HOA fee tells the whole story.

Could an El Tezal ocean view be lost to future construction?

A view visible today may change as nearby land is developed. A vacant lot in front of the terrace is not an assurance that the outlook will remain open.

Look beyond today’s photos

Visit the exact unit and check its floor, terrace orientation and sightlines. Identify vacant lots, unfinished projects and later phases in front of or beside it.

Check known plans and the applicable building limits with the appropriate professionals and authorities. Do not assume the absence of construction today means the land cannot be developed.

Ask what supports a claim of a protected view

Request the evidence behind any claim that a view is secure. BIR can help assess known neighboring development and the risk to the outlook; future views cannot be guaranteed from photographs or an MLS description.

Consider whether you would still want the condo at that price if part of the view changed. Factor that possibility into the comparison before making an offer.

What is living in El Tezal like?

El Tezal combines full-time and seasonal residents with condominium communities, houses and building lots. Shopping and everyday services around Cabo’s main corridor make it a useful area to explore, but convenience varies from street to street.

Compare the route to Costco, groceries, schools or work with the road to your actual property. Visit at the times you expect to travel. A location near Cabo San Lucas on a map does not guarantee an easy walk into town or to the beach.

For a seasonal home, ask who checks the residence while you are away. For full-time living, give water arrangements, internet, parking and storage the same attention as the view.

El Tezal condos for sale: compare the phase and the unit

Look beyond the floor plan

Compare terrace privacy, sun exposure, stairs or elevators, parking and storage. Walk from the entrance to the unit, pool and parking. An ocean or Arch view can vary by floor, orientation and neighboring construction.

Compare the full HOA responsibilities

Ask for current dues, reserves and special assessments. Review rental and pet rules, shared maintenance and facilities currently operating. Different phases of the same development can have different responsibilities and amenities.

Communities to compare

Ventanas, Vista Vela III, Vista Vela Sunset, Tramonti Paradiso, Cuatrovientos and Duara are starting points for a community comparison. Verify the exact building and unit; a development can contain completed residences alongside later phases.

Questions buyers ask about El Tezal

Is El Tezal included in the Cabo San Lucas MLS zone?

No. The MLS places El Tezal East, West and OceanSide in the Cabo Corridor. The completed-condo collection on this page includes all three labels.

Does an ocean view mean beach access?

No. Check the actual route to the beach and any access conditions separately. Visit the terrace and consider neighboring land before assuming the outlook will remain unchanged.

What should I compare for rentals?

Review the building’s rules, the management arrangements and actual income and expenses where available. Include vacancies, cleaning, utilities and shared costs rather than relying on gross revenue alone.
