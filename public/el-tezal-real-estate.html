What is living in El Tezal like?

El Tezal combines full-time and seasonal residents with condominium communities, houses and building lots. Shopping and everyday services around Cabo’s main corridor make it a useful area to explore, but convenience varies from street to street.

Compare the route to Costco, groceries, schools or work with the road to your actual property. Visit at the times you expect to travel. A location near Cabo San Lucas on a map does not guarantee an easy walk into town or to the beach.

For a seasonal home, ask who checks the residence while you are away. For full-time living, give water arrangements, internet, parking and storage the same attention as the view.

El Tezal condos for sale: compare the phase and the unit

Look beyond the floor plan

Compare terrace privacy, sun exposure, stairs or elevators, parking and storage. Walk from the entrance to the unit, pool and parking. An ocean or Arch view can vary by floor, orientation and neighboring construction.

Know what the HOA covers

Ask for current dues, reserves and special assessments. Review rental and pet rules, shared maintenance and facilities currently operating. Different phases of the same development can have different responsibilities and amenities.

Water supply and storage

Ask how the building receives water, its storage capacity, who pays for additional deliveries and how interruptions are handled. Establish whether the unit has its own reserve and which costs are shared.

Communities to compare

Ventanas, Vista Vela III, Vista Vela Sunset, Tramonti Paradiso, Cuatrovientos and Duara are starting points for a community comparison. Verify the exact building and unit; a development can contain completed residences alongside later phases.

Would a house or a building lot fit you better?

El Tezal houses

A house may give you more private outdoor space, a garage or a layout suited to full-time use. Compare water storage, maintenance, parking and any community fees. Confirm what you would be responsible for while away.

El Tezal land

Check legal access, boundaries, slope and available utilities before comparing lot prices. An elevated site may need engineering or retaining work. Verify what can be built and budget for infrastructure alongside the house.

Questions buyers ask about El Tezal

Is El Tezal included in the Cabo San Lucas MLS zone?

No. The MLS places El Tezal East and West in the Cabo Corridor. Check both community searches for the wider area.

Does an ocean view mean beach access?

No. Check the actual route to the beach and any access conditions separately. Visit the terrace and consider neighboring land before assuming the outlook will remain unchanged.

What should I compare for rentals?

Review the building’s rules, the management arrangements and actual income and expenses where available. Include vacancies, cleaning, utilities and shared costs rather than relying on gross revenue alone.
