Choosing land in San José del Cabo under $500,000

A similar asking price can buy very different sites. Compare the lot’s usable area and the full cost of your plans, alongside its community and view.

Start with community and access

Use the community filter to narrow your choices, then use More filters for the subdivision. Visit the actual route to the lot and check road condition, access arrangements and everyday driving distances. A community name alone does not establish a lot’s services or amenities.

Compare usable space, not just total size

Filter by minimum and maximum lot area in square metres, then review the survey, boundaries, slope and access. A larger lot may have less usable space than a smaller, flatter one. Ask a qualified local professional to assess the site for your intended project.

Check water and utility arrangements

Ask how water is supplied, what storage is needed and what connection or recurring charges apply. Confirm electricity, wastewater and internet arrangements for the specific lot, including any extensions or work still required.

Review ownership and building documents

Request the title and available survey and planning documents. Have your notary, attorney and design professional verify ownership, permitted use and the approvals your plans require. A listing description does not establish legal status or permission to build.

Understand community rules and fees

For a lot in a planned community, request architectural rules, dues, assessments and any construction deadlines or deposits. Confirm separately whether golf, club or beach access is included, optional or unavailable.

Budget beyond the asking price

Allow for closing costs, surveys, design, approvals, site preparation, retaining walls, utility work and construction. Check the present view from the proposed building position and ask what could be built on neighboring land.

About this San José land search

Does this include the San José Corridor?

No. This page uses the San José del Cabo MLS zone. The San José Corridor is separate. Browse San José Corridor land here.

What does under $500,000 mean?

It means a current asking price in US dollars strictly below $500,000. Closing, construction and ownership costs are additional. Clearing filters returns to all San José land within this price ceiling.

How do the lot-size filters work?

Choose minimum and maximum sizes in square metres; both limits include the size shown. For example, a minimum of 500 m² and a maximum of 1,000 m² shows reported lots in that range. Use No minimum or No maximum for an open limit. Listings without a reported lot size remain in an unfiltered search but are excluded when a size limit is selected. Confirm measurements with the property documents and a survey.

Can I still search for ocean views?

Yes. More filters includes MLS primary view within this budget. The separate ocean-view land search above includes all prices. Verify the actual outlook and future-development risk for the lot.
