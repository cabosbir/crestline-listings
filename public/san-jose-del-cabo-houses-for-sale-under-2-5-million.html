Compare San José del Cabo houses and villas beyond the asking price

Looking for San José del Cabo homes for sale? This collection includes completed houses and villas across the San José MLS zone, from lower-priced options to properties just below $2.5 million. Choose the setting and ownership responsibilities that fit your plans.

Start with the neighborhood

Compare the actual street, access and travel time to your daily destinations. If you are considering Puerto Los Cabos, Laguna Hills or another community, use the community and subdivision filters and visit the location. A San José address does not guarantee a walkable town location or beach access.

Space for living, guests and maintenance

Use the bedroom and minimum-lot-size filters to compare layouts. Look closely at stairs, guest privacy, parking, storage and outdoor space. Pools, gardens and larger residences bring maintenance responsibilities; request a realistic schedule and recent operating bills.

Confirm fees and access

Request current HOA dues, water arrangements, special assessments and the services included. If golf, a beach club or shared facilities matter to you, confirm eligibility, fees and any transfer conditions for the specific property. Ownership alone does not establish membership or access.

Inspect the house and the future view

Confirm the actual condition, utilities and operating amenities of a house marked Completed in MLS. Review neighboring parcels and ask Don about future development that could affect views or privacy. Keep a separate allowance for inspections, closing, repairs and furnishings.

About this San José del Cabo house search

Does this include the San José Corridor?

No. This search uses the San José del Cabo MLS zone, including its town and surrounding communities. MLS treats the San José Corridor as a separate zone. Browse San José Corridor houses and villas here.

What does under $2,500,000 include?

The ceiling applies to the current asking price in US dollars, strictly below $2,500,000. It does not include closing or ongoing ownership costs. All bedrooms and views are included initially. Clearing filters returns to completed houses under this ceiling; it does not remove the price limit.

Can I still look for an ocean view?

Yes. Use More filters to choose an MLS primary view within this budget, or follow the separate ocean-view search above to explore all prices. Photos, view descriptions and completion labels come from listing agents and should be confirmed against the actual property.
