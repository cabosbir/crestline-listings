What does beachfront ownership involve?

Inspect the exact coastline in front of the property. Sand, rocks, elevation, public access and swimming conditions can differ even within one area. Confirm the boundary and any applicable coastal rights or concessions with qualified professionals.

For a residence, evaluate salt exposure, exterior maintenance, storm preparation, drainage and insurance options. For land, have professionals review buildability and coastal restrictions before relying on a conceptual plan.

What can you actually build?

Start with a survey, the parcel’s legal status, permitted use and any community design requirements. Ask qualified professionals to assess setbacks, terrain, access and restrictions for the project you intend. An attractive asking price does not answer these questions.

What belongs in the full project budget?

Account for professional work, approvals, utility connections, access improvements and terrain-related construction. Compare the total estimated project and timetable with completed alternatives. Keep the land purchase decision separate from assumptions about future resale value.

Prepare for the next step

Compare suitable properties with a clear budget and intended use, then request the documents and inspections relevant to the purchase.
