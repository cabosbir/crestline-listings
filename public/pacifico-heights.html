A Pacific coast opportunity

A setting to shape
your next project.

Pacifico Heights sits on the Pacific side of Cabo San Lucas, in the San Cristóbal area near Rancho San Lucas and Diamante. Its elevated terrain opens toward the ocean, with the coastline and surrounding resort and golf landscape providing the wider setting.

This undeveloped land offers a starting point for buyers and developers who want to explore a project in Cabo’s Pacific corridor. Walk the site, study the terrain and discuss how the land could fit the houses or community you have in mind. Confirm boundaries, legal access, services, permitted use and any required approvals before relying on a development concept.

Pacifico Heights is offered as a pocket listing through Baja International Realty. Contact Don Weis directly for property information, available parcel options and a private site visit.
