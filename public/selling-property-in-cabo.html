Selling Property in Cabo San Lucas & Los Cabos

Know your realistic price, the costs of selling and what you could receive. Then choose a plan that fits your property and your priorities.

Start with evidence

What is my Cabo property worth?

A useful evaluation compares your property with recent closed sales and the alternatives a buyer can choose today. Location, views, condition, size, layout, amenities, recurring expenses and included furnishings all matter.

An asking price is an expectation. A verified sale is an outcome. Review both, and ask how differences between properties affect the recommended range. Where comparable sales are limited, the evaluation should explain that uncertainty.

Choose a price you understand

Don’s approach is to recommend a price supported by market evidence and explain your estimated net proceeds before you decide. An unrealistic price can waste valuable time and leave you with fewer options if the market weakens while the property sits.

The final decision is yours. If you want to try modestly above the advised range and can afford to wait, agree on the risks and a review date. You should know what you are testing and what would lead you to change course.

Ask your agent to explain

  • Which recent sales are genuinely comparable?
  • What competing properties will buyers tour?
  • What makes your property more or less attractive?
  • What could you receive after selling costs?
  • When will you review the response and price?

When should you consider a price adjustment?

Review inquiries, repeat visits, serious follow-up, offers and consistent buyer feedback alongside showing counts. An overpriced home can make a competing listing look better on a tour. Showings alone do not prove the price is working.

First distinguish price from other problems: weak photos, difficult showing access or missing information. Then decide whether to improve the presentation, adjust to a supported price or wait. A price reduction by itself does not establish market value.

Know your net before listing

What does it cost to sell property in Cabo?

Start with the title documents. Your agent should review your deed or fideicomiso information at the beginning of the evaluation, identify missing records and coordinate a tax study and estimated seller’s net sheet with the appropriate closing professionals.

If your agent does not want to start by reviewing your title, find another agent. A suggested listing price without an explanation of estimated costs and net proceeds leaves an important part of your decision unanswered.

Have the applicable tax calculation reviewed by the notario or a qualified Mexican tax advisor before marketing begins. The treatment depends on your property, ownership and circumstances; do not assume a standard percentage or an exemption applies to you.

Prepare before you launch

How should I prepare my home, condo or land for sale?

Make the property easy to assess

  • Clean, declutter and make rooms easy to understand in photos and on a visit.
  • Address visible maintenance and obtain estimates for larger issues before deciding what to repair.
  • Discuss known defects, past repairs and unresolved problems with your agent and legal advisor.
  • List included furniture, appliances and fixtures, plus anything you plan to exclude.
  • Agree on practical showing arrangements for occupants, tenants or guests.

Organize the seller’s file

  • Deed or fideicomiso documents and details of who must authorize the sale.
  • Property-tax, trust-fee, utility and mortgage information.
  • Relevant construction records and supporting invoices for improvements.
  • HOA dues, assessments, rules and documents available to owners.
  • Leases, reservations, deposits and management agreements where applicable.

Your closing team will identify the documents required for your particular property. Resolve missing information early.

Selling a house

Explain the layout, maintenance, water systems, parking, outdoor space and equipment included. Let buyers understand both the appeal and the cost of owning the home.

Selling a condo

Make HOA fees, assessments, amenities, parking and rental rules easy to review. Buyers are comparing the building and its ongoing obligations as well as your unit.

Selling land

Gather available surveys, access information, utility details and land-use documents. Clearly distinguish existing conditions from development ideas that still require verification.

Does rental history add value?

Documented income can help buyers assess a rental property. Prepare actual revenue and expenses for a clearly stated period, showing management, platform fees, maintenance and ownership costs. Identify owner-use periods and show financing separately. Distinguish completed income from future bookings and projections.

For some buyers, income supports an investment decision; for others, it helps offset the cost of a vacation home. Explain the evidence clearly, including any existing bookings or obligations that must be addressed in the sale.

Give buyers a clear reason to look

How will buyers find and assess my property?

Accurate presentation

Clear photography, useful descriptions and the right property details help buyers decide whether to visit. A well-made video can explain the layout and setting. Label renderings or proposed improvements clearly.

A property-specific plan

Agree on MLS exposure where appropriate, cooperation with other agents, website placement and suitable promotion. The plan should reflect the property and its likely buyers.

Useful feedback

Ask for updates on inquiries, showings and offers, with recommendations based on the response. Marketing activity matters most when it brings serious interest and useful decisions.

Is there a better season to sell in Cabo?

In Don’s local brokerage experience, October through March is generally the strongest selling season, with a reasonable window continuing through April and May. Activity typically drops in June and July, with August and September the weakest months.

Seasonality is a planning guide, not a promise. Prepare ahead of your intended launch and assess current activity for your community, property type and price range. Waiting for the usual seasonal increase does not guarantee a better result.

From evaluation to handover

What is the process for selling real estate in Los Cabos?

  1. Set the strategy

    Review your goals, title documents, comparable sales and estimated net proceeds. Agree on the asking price, listing terms, preparation and communication.

  2. Prepare and market

    Organize the documents, approve accurate photos and listing information, and make showing access practical. Review the response on an agreed schedule.

  3. Compare offers

    Consider net proceeds, buyer funding, deposits, conditions, included items and closing dates. Read the complete offer and understand the tradeoffs before accepting or countering.

  4. Work through the closing

    Your agent and closing professionals coordinate documents, agreed deadlines, outstanding issues and settlement figures. Clarify who represents you and how funds and possession will be handled.

  5. Complete the handover

    Confirm the agreed conditions for payment and possession, included items, keys and account adjustments. Keep your final sale and tax records.

Common questions from Cabo property sellers

Should I choose the agent who recommends the highest price?

Ask for the evidence behind each recommendation. Compare relevant experience, a realistic pricing explanation, estimated net proceeds and a clear marketing plan. A higher promise does not create a higher market value.

What if I am not in a hurry to sell?

You can choose to test a higher price or wait if you understand the risks. Compare carrying costs and your plans with the likely result today. Agree on a review point; improvement in the market is not guaranteed.

Should I renovate before listing?

Discuss which repairs or improvements are likely to matter to your buyers. Compare the cost and delay with the expected benefit, and obtain estimates. Do not assume every renovation will be recovered in the selling price.

How long will it take to sell?

That depends on the property, price, competition and buyer demand. Ask for relevant local evidence rather than a guaranteed timeline. Marketing time and the time needed to close an accepted offer are separate parts of the process.

Can I ask for an estimate without committing to sell?

Yes. The evaluation form lets you choose a preliminary response by email only, with no sales follow-up unless you request it, or indicate that you are open to contact for a more detailed evaluation. No phone number is required.
