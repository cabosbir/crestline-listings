What should you compare in Todos Santos?

Compare the route into town with the route to the coast. Distance, road surface and the immediate surroundings can change the experience of an otherwise similar property.

Check actual utility arrangements and nearby vacant land, especially when the property is marketed around its view or open setting.

For land in Las Tunas or farther north, compare confirmed boundaries, road access and services. Budget the complete building project rather than relying on the parcel price alone.

What can you actually build?

Start with a survey, the parcel’s legal status, permitted use and any community design requirements. Ask qualified professionals to assess setbacks, terrain, access and restrictions for the project you intend. An attractive asking price does not answer these questions.

What belongs in the full project budget?

Account for professional work, approvals, utility connections, access improvements and terrain-related construction. Compare the total estimated project and timetable with completed alternatives. Keep the land purchase decision separate from assumptions about future resale value.
