Look beyond the listing photo

Where should you buy a condo in Cabo San Lucas?

Start with an ordinary day. Do you want to walk to dinner, spend time near the beach, or have more separation from the busiest streets? Then visit the actual route between the condo and the places you will use.

Marina & downtown

Compare walking routes, street noise, parking and access to everyday shopping. Being close on a map does not tell you how an entrance, hill or busy road will affect your routine.

Beach-area condos

Check the actual access to the sand, which amenities are included and whether any club or resort access costs extra. A sea view alone does not establish beachfront ownership or convenient beach access.

Hillsides & residential areas

Compare views with the drive into town, road condition, parking and the stairs or elevator needed to reach the unit. Visit the terrace at the time of day you expect to use it.

Compare the complete picture

What will the condo cost to own?

Compare the asking price and the ongoing expenses together. Before making an offer, request the documents and estimates for the specific condo you are considering.

HOA dues & building finances

Confirm the amount, currency and payment schedule, what the dues cover, reserve funds and any approved or proposed special assessments. Review the building’s maintenance needs and meeting records.

Your own running costs

Budget for electricity, water, internet, insurance, property tax and maintenance inside the unit. Ask which services are already included in the HOA charge so you do not count them twice.

Purchase & ownership setup

Request a written closing-cost estimate for your purchase. If a bank trust is involved, include its setup and annual fees. Your closing professionals should confirm the requirements and amounts that apply.

A low asking price or low HOA charge alone does not show which condo is better value. Compare condition, services, reserve funding and recent comparable sales.

Answers before you shortlist

Questions buyers ask about Cabo condos

How much does a condo in Cabo San Lucas cost?

Use the current asking prices above to see what is available in your budget. An overall average mixes very different locations, sizes and buildings. For a buying decision, compare similar units and request recent sold comparables, alongside HOA fees and property condition.

Does “ocean view” mean I can walk to the beach?

No. An ocean-view condo may be on a hillside or separated from the beach by roads or other properties. Verify the view from the particular unit, the legal access route and the real walk or drive to the beach. MLS view labels are supplied by the listing office and should be checked during a visit.

Why start with completed condos?

A completed unit lets you inspect the actual space, building and surroundings. Don generally starts buyer shortlists with completed properties. You still need to investigate ownership documents, building condition, services and HOA finances. Completion by itself does not settle every buying question.

Can I use the condo as a vacation home and rent it when I am away?

Check the condominium’s current rules, minimum rental periods, guest policies and any applicable requirements before relying on rental income. Ask for documented expenses and rental history where available; an advertised income projection is not a guarantee. BIR represents buyers and sellers and does not offer property management.

Can foreign buyers purchase a condo here?

Foreign buyers can purchase property in Cabo through the appropriate ownership structure. Review BIR’s existing foreign ownership, buying steps and costs guide, then have your notary and legal adviser confirm the documents and requirements for the specific purchase.

How do I tell whether the asking price is realistic?

Compare recent sales of similar units, current alternatives, condition, views and ownership costs. A reduction from an earlier asking price does not establish value by itself. Read Don’s approach to the current Los Cabos market, then ask for a comparison of the properties you are considering.
