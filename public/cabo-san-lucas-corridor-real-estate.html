One Corridor, different ways to live

Which Cabo Corridor community fits your plans?

A coastal resort residence, an inland neighborhood and a golf property can offer very different daily routines. Compare where you will spend your time, the services you want and the cost of ownership alongside the asking price.

Everyday Cabo living · inland neighborhoods

El Tezal

El Tezal is a starting point for comparing condos, houses and lots on the inland side of the Cabo Corridor. It is an area of different streets and developments, rather than one community with a single set of amenities or HOA rules.

What to compare: Compare the actual drive to shopping, the marina and your regular appointments. Check road condition, water storage and nearby construction. For an ocean view, look at the land between the terrace and the bay as well as the view itself.

Condominium living · compare the individual unit

Misiones del Cabo

Misiones is a condominium option to compare when you want a coastal setting on the Cabo San Lucas side of the Corridor. Building, floor and orientation matter: two units in the same development can have different outlooks and different approaches to the pool, parking or beach.

What to compare: Walk the route from the unit, including stairs, and ask which common areas it may use. Review the condo regime’s maintenance, reserves, rental rules and any planned assessments before comparing it with a newer development.

Resort community · more than one golf experience

Cabo del Sol

Cabo del Sol combines residential neighborhoods with resorts and golf. Its official site distinguishes the private Cove Club course from the Cabo del Sol course, formerly the Desert Course. A Cabo del Sol address alone does not establish access to the private club.

What to compare: Identify the exact neighborhood and residence, then confirm the beach route and facilities included with that ownership. Separate HOA costs from club charges and verify any membership eligibility or transfer terms before relying on them.

Coastal residential setting · private club distinctions

Chileno Bay

Chileno Bay Golf & Beach Club is a private Discovery Land Company community with a Tom Fazio golf course. When comparing property around Chileno, distinguish the precise development and ownership arrangement from the broader beach or resort name.

What to compare: Ask whether the property qualifies for the club experience you want, what membership costs separately, and how family or guest access works. Verify the route to the beach and the amenities currently operating for that residence.

Separate residences · shared private-club setting

Twin Dolphin · Maravilla & Montage

Maravilla and Montage Residences are distinct residential options within the Twin Dolphin setting. Twin Dolphin describes its golf and outdoor club as private, with tee times reserved for members of the Maravilla and Montage Residences community.

What to compare: Compare the actual ownership offering, services and recurring charges for each residence. Confirm membership eligibility, transfer costs, rental restrictions and which beach or resort services your family and guests can use.

Inland golf setting · access toward Cabo San Lucas

Cabo San Lucas Country Club

The Country Club offers a golf setting to compare with the Corridor’s coastal clubs. The Los Cabos Tourism Board lists an 18-hole course and a lighted driving range, with access for visiting golfers. Playing golf here and owning a nearby property are separate decisions.

What to compare: Check the exact subdivision, its rules and the route to town. A fairway outlook is different from an ocean view; verify both on site and confirm any golf privileges or discounts directly rather than assuming they come with a purchase.

Beach access, shopping and everyday life

How will you reach the beach?

Santa María is one of the Corridor’s well-known coves for swimming and snorkeling. Being near the bay does not tell you how to reach the sand from a particular property. Walk or drive the real route and distinguish a beach visit from access to a private club’s loungers, pools or services.

Conditions change: follow beach flags and local guidance on the day. An oceanfront address does not establish that the water in front of it is suitable for swimming.

Beach context: Los Cabos Tourism Board: Santa María ↗

Which side of the highway suits you?

Compare both the outbound and return trip to groceries, restaurants, medical appointments and the marina. An entrance on the opposite side of the highway can change the route. Test the drive at the time you expect to use it rather than relying on a straight-line distance.

For an inland property, include the final streets and driveway in that check. For a resort residence, include the trip from its entrance to the actual unit. Check deliveries, guest access and parking as part of the same visit.

What will the property feel like year-round?

Visit the terrace in the afternoon as well as the morning. Shade, orientation, wind exposure and road or construction noise can affect how much you use the outdoor space. Ask how water is supplied and stored, and confirm internet service at the address if you plan to work from home.

A seasonal residence also needs a plan for maintenance while you are away: who checks the property, manages access and responds when a repair is needed?

Will the ocean view remain?

A photograph records today’s outlook. A lower lot, an undeveloped parcel or a future phase can change what is visible. Ask Don to assess the actual view, neighboring development potential and the questions to resolve before you rely on it.

Compare views from the living room and bedrooms as well as the terrace. A higher floor or hillside position can help the outlook, but neither by itself guarantees protection.

Compare the complete ownership picture

What should you budget beyond the purchase price?

Condos
Request current dues, the approved budget, reserves and planned assessments for the actual building or phase. Confirm what is included, along with parking, storage, pet and rental rules. An elevator, shared pool and extensive services create a different budget from a simpler condominium.
Houses & Villas
Separate any community dues from the cost of maintaining the house, pool and garden. Review cooling, water storage, roof and exterior condition, insurance and management while you are away. In a branded residence, establish which services are required and what they cost.
Land & building lots
Before comparing a lot with a completed residence, obtain professional estimates for design, approvals, terrain work, access and utilities. Confirm permitted use, setbacks and community design requirements. The lot’s asking price is only one part of the budget and timetable.
Golf, beach clubs and resort services
Ask for written membership eligibility, initiation or transfer charges, annual dues and guest rules. Confirm whether any membership transfers with the sale. Treat promised access separately from verified rights that apply to the property.

Why are we showing only completed properties?

We start with completed properties so you can inspect the actual residence, compare its condition and surroundings, and see which services and amenities are operating. This reduces exposure to construction delays, unfinished amenities and a developer’s need for future sales or progress payments to finish the project.

A completed label is not a guarantee: confirm the property’s condition, documents, utilities and any HOA obligations. We consider preconstruction selectively, with particular attention to the developer’s record of completing projects and financial capacity to deliver through slower sales or rising costs.

Questions buyers ask about Cabo Corridor real estate

Is the Cabo San Lucas Corridor the same as the entire Tourist Corridor?

No. The Tourist Corridor connects the two towns. This page’s listings use the Cabo Corridor MLS zone; the San José Corridor is a separate selection. Use our Tourist Corridor overview to compare the two sides, or open the San José Corridor listings.

Should I choose El Tezal or a coastal community?

Start with your everyday routine and the amenities you will actually use. Compare an El Tezal property’s streets, services and individual HOA with the access, membership and service arrangements of the coastal residence. A view and an asking price are not enough to establish equivalent ownership costs or convenience.

Can a beach or golf club membership be assumed from the listing?

No. Ask which club is meant, whether the property qualifies, whether membership is available, and which charges and restrictions apply. Public beach access, hotel facilities and private club membership are separate questions.

Can I rent out a Corridor condo or house between visits?

Check the rules for the specific condominium regime, community and any branded-residence agreement. Confirm minimum stays, guest access, management requirements and current local obligations before relying on rental income. A neighboring property being advertised for rent does not establish permission for yours.

How do I compare two properties with similar prices?

Put their recurring costs, condition, usable space, view, parking and access side by side. Add any near-term repairs or assessments, and separate included amenities from optional memberships. Send Don the MLS numbers and your intended use so he can help identify the differences that matter to you.
