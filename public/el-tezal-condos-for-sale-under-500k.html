What should you compare in an El Tezal condo under $500,000?

A lower asking price is only part of the comparison. Look at the actual residence, the building’s shared responsibilities and what is operating today. Two condos at the same price can have very different monthly costs.

Water and the monthly HOA bill

For shared condominium water supplies, trucked-water costs are paid through the HOA and allocated to owners. Ask whether they are included in regular dues or billed separately each month. Request recent bills, the allocation method, delivery arrangements and details of storage and pumps.

Compare the total monthly cost, including water, regular dues and any special assessments. Do not assume that an advertised HOA amount includes every charge.

Check today’s view—and what could change

An ocean view can vary between floors and phases. Visit the actual terrace, identify neighboring vacant lots and ask Don to assess how future construction could affect the outlook. A photograph or the MLS view label does not establish that a view is protected.

Two or three bedrooms?

Use the bedroom filter to compare layouts for full-time living, guests or a home office. “2 or more” also includes three-bedroom units. Check usable interior space, terrace privacy, parking, stairs or elevators and the route to everyday services.

Why completed condos?

You can inspect the actual unit and see which services and amenities operate now. A completed condo can still be in an unfinished phase: confirm access, utilities, shared facilities and the HOA’s finances before committing.

Which locations are included?

This collection includes MLS communities El Tezal-East, El Tezal-West and El Tezal-OceanSide within the Cabo Corridor zone. It does not include all Cabo San Lucas or all Corridor condos. Confirm the actual location and route to shops, work and the beach.

Does under $500,000 include closing and ownership costs?

No. The ceiling applies to the current asking price in US dollars, strictly below $500,000. Keep a separate budget for closing, furnishings or repairs and ongoing ownership costs. Ask BIR for a property-specific comparison.
