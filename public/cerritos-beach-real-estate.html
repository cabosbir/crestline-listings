Cerritos Beach: Come for the Waves, Stay for the Way of Life

By Matthew (Matt) Rosenberg, BIR agent and year-round El Pescadero resident.

Some places ask you to fill your itinerary. Cerritos Beach makes a convincing argument for leaving it open. A morning by the water, a surf lesson, fish tacos, then an evening watching the Pacific change colors: it is easy to start imagining a longer stay.

Cerritos sits on the Pacific coast near El Pescadero, south of Todos Santos. For a buyer, the appeal is more than a holiday photograph. The question is how beach life would fit around work, shopping, visiting friends and the ordinary days between outings.

Start with the surf

You do not have to arrive with a board under your arm. Mario Surf School ↗ offers private and group lessons at Cerritos. If surfing is not your thing, walk the sand or watch the lineup. A beach morning can be worthwhile without a packed schedule.

Check current conditions and seek local guidance before entering the Pacific. The La Paz Tourism Board ↗ distinguishes Cerritos from nearby San Pedrito, where strong currents and a rocky bottom require more experience. A surfing reputation does not establish safe swimming conditions on a particular day.

A beach day, then something good to eat

Barracuda Cantina ↗ is a family-run, open-air spot at Cerritos with fish and shrimp tacos, ceviche and drinks. Check its current announcements if music is part of your plan.

Nearby Pescadero offers a change of setting. Hierbabuena ↗ combines garden-inspired cooking with seasonal ingredients and wood-oven pizza. Agricole ↗ offers coffee, bread, produce and strawberries and cream, plus a country kitchen with its own schedule. These stops help you picture the life around a property, not simply the rooms inside it.

Which version of Cerritos life fits you?

A weekend getaway, a full-time house and a place to work remotely call for different priorities. Could you see yourself making time for the beach before work? Would you enjoy a quiet evening at home? Would trips to Pescadero for food and Todos Santos for galleries give you the balance you want?

Visit the approach roads and walk the access route you would actually use. Carrying a surfboard or groceries can change what feels like an easy distance. An ocean view and convenient beach access are different features. Confirm water arrangements, internet service, privacy and maintenance at the exact address.

If renting the property is part of your plan, check the rules and actual operating costs before relying on income. The aim is to connect the lifestyle you want with a property that works for it. Life’s better down here. Let’s find your place in it.

Choose the property that fits

What should you compare in Cerritos Beach?

Condos near the beach

Compare completed units, HOA finances, parking, building upkeep and rental rules. Confirm whether pools and other amenities are operating and included in your ownership costs.

Houses for longer stays

Think about groceries, shade, outdoor living, water storage and the drive you would make for everyday errands. Inspect the house and access at different times of day.

Land & future building

Investigate title, boundaries, permitted use, access and the cost of connecting or providing services. A low lot price does not establish the final cost of a usable home.

What should you check on a Cerritos property visit?

  • The actual view: see it from the rooms and terrace you will use. Ask Don to assess surrounding land and the possibility of future development affecting it; a current photograph cannot guarantee a lasting view.
  • The building in operation: for a completed condo, inspect the unit and common areas. Request the current HOA budget, reserves, any special assessments, rental rules and a clear list of amenities already operating.
  • Access and upkeep: drive the final approach, check parking and delivery access, and ask about road maintenance. Inspect exposed metalwork, windows and outdoor equipment as part of a professional property inspection.
  • A realistic ownership budget: compare documented HOA charges, utilities, insurance, routine maintenance and care during absences. If rental income is part of your plan, request actual records and expenses rather than relying on a projection.

Questions to answer before buying

What services does this specific property have?

Ask how water is supplied, whether service is connected or only proposed, what storage is installed and which electricity and internet options are available at the exact address.

How does Cerritos compare with El Pescadero and Todos Santos?

Compare the beach access you want with your day-to-day trips for shopping, dining and services. Visit all three areas if you are unsure; the best fit depends on your routine and the particular property.

Should I buy a completed condo or preconstruction?

Start by comparing completed alternatives you can inspect. For a proposed project, investigate delivery history, construction funding, contract terms and what happens if sales or construction slow. Ask for evidence specific to that project.
