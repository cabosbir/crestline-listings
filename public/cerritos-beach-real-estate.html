Where is Cerritos Beach, and how does it compare?

Cerritos is part of the Pacific beach area around El Pescadero, south of Todos Santos. The coast is known for surfing; Pescadero provides a different town setting, while Todos Santos adds a historic center and cultural attractions.

Use these as three places to compare during a viewing trip. Start with Cerritos if regular beach visits are a priority, include Pescadero to compare your daily errands, and visit Todos Santos if being close to the town center matters. The exact address and route will determine which suits you.

Area background: Los Cabos Tourism Board on Pescadero ↗ and Visit Baja Sur on Todos Santos ↗.

Surfing and beach access are separate questions

The La Paz Tourism Board distinguishes Cerritos, which attracts beginning and intermediate surfers, from San Pedrito, where strong currents and a rocky bottom call for more experience. A surfing reputation does not establish safe swimming conditions on a particular day. Check current conditions and local guidance before entering the water.

Beach information: La Paz Tourism Board on Cerritos and San Pedrito ↗.

For a property purchase, walk the route from the entrance to the beach. Confirm the access you would use, any separate arrangements and whether carrying boards or beach equipment changes the practical distance. An ocean view and convenient beach access are different features.

What should you compare in Cerritos Beach?

Condos near the beach

Compare completed units, HOA finances, parking, building upkeep and rental rules. Confirm whether pools and other amenities are operating and included in your ownership costs.

Houses for longer stays

Think about groceries, shade, outdoor living, water storage and the drive you would make for everyday errands. Inspect the house and access at different times of day.

Land & future building

Investigate title, boundaries, permitted use, access and the cost of connecting or providing services. A low lot price does not establish the final cost of a usable home.

What should you check on a Cerritos property visit?

  • The actual view: see it from the rooms and terrace you will use. Ask Don to assess surrounding land and the possibility of future development affecting it; a current photograph cannot guarantee a lasting view.
  • The building in operation: for a completed condo, inspect the unit and common areas. Request the current HOA budget, reserves, any special assessments, rental rules and a clear list of amenities already operating.
  • Access and upkeep: drive the final approach, check parking and delivery access, and ask about road maintenance. Inspect exposed metalwork, windows and outdoor equipment as part of a professional property inspection.
  • A realistic ownership budget: compare documented HOA charges, utilities, insurance, routine maintenance and care during absences. If rental income is part of your plan, request actual records and expenses rather than relying on a projection.

Questions to answer before buying

What services does this specific property have?

Ask how water is supplied, whether service is connected or only proposed, what storage is installed and which electricity and internet options are available at the exact address.

How does Cerritos compare with El Pescadero and Todos Santos?

Compare the beach access you want with your day-to-day trips for shopping, dining and services. Visit all three areas if you are unsure; the best fit depends on your routine and the particular property.

Should I buy a completed condo or preconstruction?

Start by comparing completed alternatives you can inspect. For a proposed project, investigate delivery history, construction funding, contract terms and what happens if sales or construction slow. Ask for evidence specific to that project.
