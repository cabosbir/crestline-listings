Choose the setting that fits your routine

Palmilla, Querencia, Cabo Real, Casa del Mar or El Dorado?

A private club residence, a beach-area condo and a house overlooking a public-play golf course offer different experiences. Compare the services you will use, the membership you want and the trips you will make before treating similar asking prices as equivalent.

Coastal neighborhoods · beach and golf

Palmilla

Palmilla is worth comparing when beach time and a San José base are priorities. The Tourism Board identifies Palmilla’s sheltered cove as a swimming and snorkeling destination, while Palmilla Golf Club lists three nine-hole layouts: Ocean, Mountain and Arroyo. The beach, golf club, hotel and individual residential neighborhoods are different parts of the ownership picture.

What to compare: Identify the exact neighborhood and the route from the residence to the beach. Ask which facilities it can use, what costs extra and whether golf access requires a separate arrangement. Do not assume that a Palmilla address includes One&Only hotel services.

Private club community · hillside and beach experiences

Querencia

Querencia brings together Tom Fazio golf, a clubhouse and a separate beach-club experience. Its official club information describes member facilities that include dining, fitness, pools and racquet sports. Compare the residence’s position within the community with where you expect to spend your day.

What to compare: Confirm membership eligibility and current availability for the property, along with initiation or transfer charges, recurring dues and guest rules. Test the trip from the residence to both the clubhouse and beach club; a view from a terrace does not mean the beach is a walk away.

Golf and residential setting · public tee times

Cabo Real

Cabo Real’s Robert Trent Jones Jr. course runs through coastal and desert terrain. Its operator, Questro Golf, welcomes public play subject to availability. This offers a different starting point from a members-only golf community when comparing where to live and where to play.

What to compare: Check the particular subdivision, its HOA budget and its beach route. Verify any owner golf rate or playing privilege directly. A property overlooking the course, a membership and a booked round of golf are separate things.

Condominium choice · compare the unit and regime

Casa del Mar

Casa del Mar is a condo option to compare within the broader Cabo Real area. Start with the actual unit: its building, floor, orientation and usable terrace can matter more to your daily experience than a general resort description.

What to compare: Walk the route to the sand and the amenities shown in the listing. Ask which pools and services belong to the condominium, which are hotel facilities, and what use is permitted. Review the building’s reserves, assessments, rental rules, parking and maintenance history.

Members-only setting · golf and beach club

El Dorado

El Dorado is a private Discovery Land Company community with a Jack Nicklaus golf course, beach club and member services. Its property offerings include residences and custom homesites, so compare a finished property with the full cost and timetable of building before deciding between them.

What to compare: Obtain the current membership and ownership terms for the exact property. Confirm transfer conditions, family and guest access, recurring charges and any construction requirements. Advertised community amenities do not, by themselves, establish what transfers in a resale.

Beach access and everyday life on the San José side

Which beach will you actually use?

Palmilla’s cove is a recognized swimming and snorkeling destination. That does not make every nearby shoreline suitable for swimming or every property a short walk from the beach. Visit the access point you intend to use and follow local flags and conditions on the day.

Separate the route to the sand from access to a hotel pool, beach-club services or private amenities. Ask how children, visiting family and rental guests may use each facility.

Beach context: Los Cabos Tourism Board: Palmilla Beach ↗

How often will you go into San José?

Test the drive to your groceries, restaurants and regular appointments, including the return trip. Highway crossings, turning points and the distance inside a gated community can change the practical route. For frequent flights, test the airport route from the property rather than relying on a general area estimate.

If walking to the historic center is a priority, compare the San José town guide alongside this Corridor selection.

Will the residence work while you are away?

A condo with shared maintenance and a house with its own pool require different arrangements. Establish who handles keys, inspections, repairs and storm preparation. Confirm internet service at the address and ask about water supply, storage and any backup systems.

For a serviced residence, request the actual service agreement. Check which charges are mandatory even if you only visit for part of the year.

Confirm the view and its future

Compare the outlook from the rooms and terrace you will use, not only the best listing photograph. Ask Don to assess nearby vacant land, future phases and other development that could affect it.

A golf or ocean view today does not by itself establish long-term protection. Identify the specific land and applicable restrictions before relying on a seller’s assurance.

What belongs in your ownership budget?

Condominium or community costs
Compare current dues, what they cover, approved budgets, reserves and planned assessments. Confirm whether a residence pays both a building or neighborhood charge and a wider community charge.
Club membership and services
Request written terms for eligibility, availability, transfer or initiation charges, annual dues and guest access. Establish whether golf, beach facilities and other services use the same membership or separate arrangements.
Condition, maintenance and management
Include inspections and near-term work on cooling, roof, water systems, exterior finishes and any private pool. If you plan to rent between visits, review the exact rental rules and management obligations before counting on income.
A lot versus a completed residence
Obtain professional estimates for site work, utility connections, design and approvals. Check the community’s design rules and construction obligations. Compare the full project budget and timetable with a finished property you can inspect.

Why are we showing only completed properties?

We start with completed properties so you can inspect the actual residence, compare its condition and surroundings, and see which services and amenities are operating. This reduces exposure to construction delays, unfinished amenities and a developer’s need for future sales or progress payments to finish the project.

A completed label is not a guarantee: confirm the property’s condition, documents, utilities and any HOA obligations. We consider preconstruction selectively, with particular attention to the developer’s record of completing projects and financial capacity to deliver through slower sales or rising costs.

San José Corridor property questions

Is this the same search as San José del Cabo?

No. This collection uses the San Jose Corridor MLS zone. Town, the Cabo San Lucas side of the Corridor and East Cape have their own guides and selections. Check the property’s map position as well as its MLS community label.

Should a non-golfer consider a golf community?

Possibly, if the residence, other amenities and location fit your plans. First establish whether membership is required, which type is available and what it costs. Compare the complete annual expense with a property outside a club community.

Does a condo near a hotel include the hotel’s amenities?

Do not assume so. Ask for the rights attached to that specific unit and any separate service or membership agreement. Confirm how owner, family, guest and renter access differ.

Can I search only for ocean-view houses or condos?

Yes. Use the ocean-view searches above, then narrow by price and location. Listings depend on information entered by listing agents; photos and a property visit help establish the actual outlook, and Don can help assess future development concerns.
