What should you compare in an El Tezal house under $750,000?

Looking for El Tezal homes for sale? Compare the space you will use every day as well as the asking price. A completed house lets you inspect the actual layout, outdoor areas and services before deciding.

Three bedrooms—and room for your routine

Use the bedroom filter to find three or more bedrooms, then compare usable interior space, a home-office option, parking and outdoor privacy. Check stairs, storage and the route to shopping, work and schools. A bedroom count alone does not tell you how well the house will suit you.

Understand who pays for water

Ask how this particular house receives water, how often deliveries are needed, and who pays. A standalone house may arrange and pay for its own trucked water. Where supply is shared, costs may be included in HOA dues or billed separately to owners.

Request recent bills and inspect storage, pumps and any filtration equipment. Compare the total cost, including regular dues and special assessments, rather than assuming an advertised HOA figure includes water.

Check the view and neighboring lots

Look from the actual terrace and living areas. Identify vacant neighboring land and ask Don to assess how future construction could affect the view and privacy. MLS view descriptions and photos should be checked against the property.

Completed house, operating community

These listings are marked Completed in MLS. Confirm the house’s condition, utilities and access, and check which shared facilities actually operate. A finished residence can still be within an unfinished phase. Ask who maintains roads, gates and shared equipment.

The ceiling applies to the current asking price in US dollars, strictly below $750,000. It does not include closing or ongoing ownership costs. All bedrooms and views are included initially. Clearing filters returns to completed houses under this ceiling; it does not remove the price limit.
