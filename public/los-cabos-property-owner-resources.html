Los Cabos Property Owner Resources

Official property-tax and mapping tools, with practical instructions for using them.

Property-tax statementGovernment mapUsing English translation

Los Cabos property-tax statement (Predial)

Look up the municipal property-tax account using the property's clave catastral (cadastral identification number). Useful for owners checking their account, sellers gathering documents and buyers reviewing a property.

Enter the clave catastral and select Buscar (Search) to view the account statement. If browser translation displays “Enter password,” enter the property’s cadastral identification number in that field.

BCS government map

Explore the Los Cabos area with the state government's interactive mapping tool. Owners, sellers and buyers can use it as a reference when researching locations and surrounding areas.

The map opens centered on Los Cabos. Zoom in and explore the available layers. For a purchase or sale, have the property's legal boundaries confirmed separately.

Open the government map ↗

Can I use these tools in English?

Both government tools open in a new tab, keeping BIR available. Use your browser’s Translate option to read them in English.

For the property-tax lookup, clave catastral means the property’s cadastral identification number. If automatic translation displays “Enter password,” use the cadastral number in that field.

Keep a copy of the statement with your property records. For questions about an account balance, payment or map record, confirm the information with the relevant government office.
