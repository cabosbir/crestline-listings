Why are we showing only completed properties?

We start with completed properties so you can inspect the actual residence, compare its condition and surroundings, and see which services and amenities are operating. This reduces exposure to construction delays, unfinished amenities and a developer’s need for future sales or progress payments to finish the project.

A completed label is not a guarantee: confirm the property’s condition, documents, utilities and any HOA obligations. We consider preconstruction selectively, with particular attention to the developer’s record of completing projects and financial capacity to deliver through slower sales or rising costs.

Which location fits the way you will use your condo?

Compare convenience as carefully as scenery. For frequent marina visits or dinners in town, check the actual route from the building. A short distance on a map can involve hills, stairs, traffic or parking that changes your daily experience.

Use the Cabo San Lucas area guide and Pedregal guide to plan your search. If you are also considering El Tezal, it is listed under the Cabo Corridor zone in our six-zone condo collection.

For a closer building comparison, explore Las Cascadas floor plans and terraces or the Cabo marina condo guide. Those community guides include their own listings; confirm the view of each condo individually.

For a winter residence, compare elevator access, parking, storage and ease of leaving the unit between visits. For full-time living, also consider grocery trips, internet service, utilities and everyday noise.

Shortlist the condo you will actually use

Compare the view from the sofa, dining area and terrace

Two condos with the same MLS view label can offer different outlooks. Compare photographs taken from the actual unit, then use a visit or a current walkthrough video to check these three points.

Inside the condo

Check the outlook from the places where you will sit, cook or dine. Ask whether the listing’s main view photo was taken inside the condo, on its terrace, from the roof or in a shared area.

For an Arch view, locate the Arch from the actual living space. For a marina view, compare the harbor outlook and nearby activity with what you want from an open-water view.

On the terrace

Ask for indoor area and terrace area separately, including the units of measurement. Compare space for chairs and a table, covered versus open areas, privacy, sun exposure and access from the rooms.

At Las Cascadas, use the individual unit plans to compare layouts, then confirm the listing’s unit number and actual view.

Beyond the railing

Identify nearby buildings, vacant lots and future phases that sit within the sightline. Ask what evidence supports any claim that the view will remain open.

Check the separate route to the beach or marina. A broad ocean view does not tell you the walking distance, access rights or swimming conditions.

Send Don two or three MLS numbers and describe the outlook you want. Include whether you need two bedrooms, a dining terrace, an elevator or a particular beach route; these details make the comparison more useful than a view label alone.

What should you compare besides the purchase price?

Request current HOA dues, the approved budget, reserve information and any announced assessments. Confirm which services are included and which remain your responsibility. Ocean exposure, shared elevators, pools and landscaping can create maintenance needs that are not obvious from listing photographs.

Review the rules for pets, guests and any intended rentals for the specific condominium regime and phase. Check operating amenities, parking and storage rights, and the condition of the building as well as the unit.

Use our buying-cost guide to prepare a complete budget. If rental use matters, ask for documented results and expenses for the property; a view alone does not establish income or returns.

Questions about buying an ocean-view condo

Are these beachfront condos?

Some listings may be described as oceanfront, but this collection also includes ocean, Arch and marina views. Select a primary-view category and verify the exact beach location and access for any condo you consider.

Why are only completed condos shown?

You can inspect the actual unit, its view, the building and the amenities operating today. This avoids relying solely on a planned delivery. Completed properties still require document review and inspections; the explanation above outlines our approach to preconstruction.

Can I compare ocean-view condos at different prices?

Yes. Choose a price band, minimum bedrooms and a view, then switch between lowest-to-highest and highest-to-lowest asking prices. Compare HOA costs, unit condition and the specific outlook before deciding whether a price offers value.

How should I compare terrace sizes?

Ask for indoor area and deck or terrace area separately, with the units stated. Confirm the measurements and layout for the condo being sold, including covered versus open space where reported. Compare usable dining space, privacy, shade and exposure; total area alone does not tell you how the terrace will feel.

Does this search include El Tezal ocean-view condos?

This page covers the Cabo San Lucas MLS zone. El Tezal belongs to the Cabo Corridor; use the El Tezal ocean-view search or the broader Cabo Corridor water-view collection to compare those locations.

Can BIR help me arrange showings?

Yes. Send Don your favorites or MLS numbers, budget, planned use and visit dates. He can help compare suitable properties and arrange the next steps.
