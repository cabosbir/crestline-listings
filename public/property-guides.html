Cabo San Lucas

Start with the way you want to spend a day in Cabo: close to the marina and restaurants, above town in Pedregal, or near the beach. A Cabo San Lucas address alone does not tell you whether daily trips will be walkable or involve a steep drive.

San José del Cabo

Decide whether your priority is town, the hotel-zone beach area, Costa Azul or a planned community such as Club Campestre or Puerto Los Cabos. These locations should be compared by the way you will live there, not simply under one San José address.

Cerritos Beach

For Cerritos, start by comparing the exact relationship between the property and the beach. A hillside ocean outlook, a beach-area condo and a parcel farther inland have different access, service and maintenance considerations.

El Pescadero

El Pescadero buyers should compare the village, agricultural surroundings and coast as distinct settings. Quiet space and a Pacific outlook can appeal for daily living, but the exact access and service arrangements are central to the choice.

Todos Santos

Todos Santos offers a different comparison from the Cabo resort corridors: town access, residential neighborhoods toward Las Tunas and settings farther north. Choose the daily-living pattern first, then compare properties within it.
