What draws buyers to Todos Santos?

Todos Santos is a Pueblo Mágico with a historic town center, art galleries and restaurants. Visit Baja Sur highlights its mission, palm groves and theater by the main square. The town’s cultural life gives buyers a different starting point from choosing a property mainly for beach access.

Town background: Visit Baja Sur’s Todos Santos guide ↗.

Start a viewing trip in the town center

The municipal tourism directory identifies the Nuestra Señora del Pilar mission, the Manuel Márquez de León theater and the Néstor Agúndez cultural center. Use the center as a reference point for comparing your actual route to cafés, galleries and the places you expect to visit regularly.

Local landmarks: La Paz municipal tourism directory for Todos Santos ↗.

Compare a town address with a coastal setting

A property advertised as Todos Santos may offer a very different routine from one close to the central streets. For each shortlist, compare the exact map location, road surface, parking and the route back to town. What should you compare in Todos Santos?

Houses near town

Visit at different times to understand nearby activity, parking and access. Inspect the building’s condition and ask what is included in the sale and what maintenance is likely to be needed.

Coastal & outlying houses

Verify access, utilities and the route to everyday services. Compare the outdoor spaces, exposure and view from the specific house, and investigate what may be built nearby.

Land for a future home

Confirm title, boundaries, access and permitted use before committing to a design. Budget for site preparation and services as well as the purchase price, and check the requirements for the intended construction.

How should you compare Todos Santos properties?

  • A walk you would actually make: if town living is the attraction, walk from the property to your usual stops, including after dark. Check the route, crossings, lighting and parking yourself.
  • A house inspection suited to the building: for an older property, ask the inspector about roof condition, moisture, electrical and plumbing systems and past alterations. Check any intended renovation with the appropriate local professionals.
  • The tradeoff behind an ocean view: compare the outlook with the drive to town, road access and services. Ask Don about neighboring land and possible development that could change the view.
  • The cost of your intended use: a full-time residence, a seasonal house and a rental property need different budgets. Request actual maintenance and utility records, and verify any rental restrictions before relying on income estimates.

Questions to answer before buying

Should I compare Todos Santos with Cerritos and El Pescadero?

Yes, if you are still choosing your setting. Compare proximity to town, the beach routes you would use, property types and services. A visit to all three can clarify your priorities.

Are condo and home ownership costs the same?

A condo may include shared maintenance through HOA dues, while a house owner may arrange more services individually. Ask for the current fees, bills and maintenance needs of each property you are comparing.

How do I judge an asking price here?

Request recent sold comparables with similar location, property type, condition and access to services. Broad market averages or a reduction from an old asking price do not establish the value of the individual property.
