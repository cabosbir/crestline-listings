Private gardens. In-town convenience.

A home that lives
up to its name.

Casa Oasis is offered as a pocket listing through Baja International Realty. Behind private garage gates, a completely walled tropical courtyard creates a peaceful setting for a home in town.

At the heart of this 3-bedroom, 2-bath home is a secluded courtyard surrounded by mature palms and lush landscaping. A private swimming pool, generous deck space and several areas for relaxing or entertaining make outdoor living part of every day. There is room to grow your own food and herbs, too.

By evening, landscape lighting and the illuminated pool turn the courtyard into a private retreat in the middle of Cabo.

Warm Baja character

Comfort inside.
The courtyard just outside.

High ceilings, arched windows and doors, marble flooring and extensive custom wood cabinetry give the home its welcoming character. The open kitchen features granite countertops, stainless-steel appliances, gas cooking and a large center island.

The living area features two comfortable white leather couches and a 100-inch TV with a Bose soundbar for movie nights, and opens naturally toward the pool and gardens. Bedrooms have built-in cabinetry, ceiling fans and mini-split air conditioning. A dedicated laundry area and abundant storage support comfortable everyday living.

Cabo close at hand

Privacy without
giving up convenience.

Casa Oasis is approximately 1.4 miles—about a five-minute drive—from the Cabo San Lucas Marina and major grocery stores. La Comer is about five minutes away, with Chedraui, Soriana and many additional shopping options nearby.

Beginning just two blocks from the house, the surrounding neighborhood offers sushi, tacos, OXXO, a neighborhood market, a gym, a pharmacy, a gas station and other everyday conveniences. The home itself remains tucked away on a quiet dead-end street.

Private pool. Walled tropical gardens. No HOA dues. The convenience of living in town, with the feeling of your own secluded retreat.
